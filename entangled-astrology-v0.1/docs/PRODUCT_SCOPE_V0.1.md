# Product Scope v0.1

## Brand hierarchy

- **Entangled** — umbrella consumer ecosystem.
- **Entangled Astrology** — this native Android application.
- **Entangled Oracle** — calculation, interpretation, and report engine.

## Primary navigation

Today · Timeline · Chart · Places · Library

## Consumer promise

Entangled Astrology is an inspectable map of astrological time: what is active, how patterns combine, when they peak, and why the interpretation says what it says.

## Excluded from v0.1

- fabricated demonstration readings,
- token/credit economy,
- central AI chatbot,
- cloud account system,
- payment processing,
- unverified geocoding,
- a second astrology engine written for the app.
