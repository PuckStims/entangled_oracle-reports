III. Reading this forecast

How to Use This Book

You do not need to read this report from beginning to end. Start with the Year Orientation, return to the current Monthly Chapter as the year arrives, and use the Cycle Ledger or Technical Appendix whenever you want the astrological detail behind a pattern.

Timing is a condition, not a command.

Dated windows describe changing symbolic conditions and concentration points. They do not guarantee a single external event.

Long cycles matter more than one day.

An exact contact is often a peak inside a larger cycle that may already be active, developing, or still integrating.

Quiet is not unimportant.

A softer month can be restorative, preparatory, private, or structurally necessary. A quieter chapter can still be meaningful.

Your life remains part of the meaning.

Relationships, material reality, health, choice, context, and capacity shape how any forecast condition is lived.

XII. Technical appendix

Terms, Methods & Definitions

Transit

A moving planet's relationship to a natal placement or chart angle.

Exact contact

The closest point of an aspect inside a larger timing window.

Orb

The allowable distance from exactness used to define an aspect's timing range.

Station

The point at which a planet appears to pause before turning retrograde or direct.

Retrograde

An apparent backward motion from Earth's perspective that often marks review, revision, or reworking in timing language.

House ingress

A planet entering a new Whole Sign house, shifting the life territory through which it is expressed.

Eclipse

A solar or lunar event that may concentrate attention around a natal point or area of life.

Whole Sign houses

A house system in which the entire sign containing the Ascendant is the first house.

Activity levels

Reader-facing labels for relative symbolic concentration; they are not predictions of guaranteed outcomes.
