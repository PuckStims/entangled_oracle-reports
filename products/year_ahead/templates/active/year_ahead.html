X. Technical appendix

Terms, Methods & Definitions

Transit

A moving planet's relationship to a natal placement or chart angle.

Exact contact

The closest point of an aspect inside a larger timing window.

Orb

The allowable distance from exactness used to define an aspect's timing range.

Station

The point at which a planet appears to pause before turning retrograde or direct.

Retrograde

An apparent backward motion from Earth's perspective that often marks review, revision, or reworking in timing language.

House ingress

A planet entering a new Whole Sign house, shifting the life territory through which it is expressed.

Eclipse

A solar or lunar event that may concentrate attention around a natal point or area of life.

Whole Sign houses

A house system in which the entire sign containing the Ascendant is the first house.

Activity levels

Reader-facing labels for relative symbolic concentration; they are not predictions of guaranteed outcomes.

Landmark

A sustained, high-priority transit cycle used here as a major turning point within the forecast year.

Forecast shape

A structural label describing how concentration is distributed across the year's existing monthly intensity pattern.

Method note

This forecast is calculated with the Swiss Ephemeris using the {{ methodology_label | default('Tropical zodiac + Whole Sign houses') }} methodology. The report draws on natal transits, Whole Sign house ingresses, planetary stations, and eclipses that contact selected natal targets. It does not claim to predict one fixed future; it maps changing symbolic conditions, timing concentrations, and recurring developmental patterns. The proprietary EO material functions here as an additional interpretive lens layered onto that astrological base, not a replacement for it.
