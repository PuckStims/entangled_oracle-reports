How This Report Fits The Suite

Internal Architecture is your operating-map report within the Entangled Oracle suite. It describes how signal, choice, energy, boundary, contact, pressure, and restoration tend to organize in your system. Later forecasts and state snapshots can refer back to this architecture when timing activates specific registers.

How To Use This Report

This report works best as a reference map you return to over time, not as a one-time label. It describes patterns to observe, test, and refine through lived context.
