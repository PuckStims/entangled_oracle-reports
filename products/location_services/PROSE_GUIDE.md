# EO Locational Prose Guide

**Status:** Content-lead prose guidance for Location Services.  
**Scope:** How Place Resonance and later locational products should sound, what they may claim, and what they must avoid.
**Primary editorial references:** `products/location_services/CONTENT_REFERENCES.md`, `products/location_services/references/Entangled_Oracle_Master_Plainspeak_Guide_v5.md`, and `products/location_services/references/Entangled_Oracle_Anti-Monotony_Redundancy_Protocol_v2.md`.

## Voice Standard

Location Services should sound like evidence-aware symbolic place analysis. The tone is warm, direct, adult, and precise. It should not sound like a travel horoscope, a relocation sales funnel, or a list of planetary keywords.

This guide extends the Master Plainspeak Guide and Anti-Monotony Protocol into locational work. When this guide is silent, those two reference documents govern prose decisions.

## Confidence Standard

Location Services is a perspective service. It is not enough to name
symbolism politely and stop there. A locational report should help the
reader understand what a place is more likely to emphasize, reward,
pressure, expose, support, or complicate.

That means the prose must be willing to pick a side when the evidence
actually converges.

Good:

```text
This place is more supportive of visibility than restoration.
The chart evidence is stronger for public participation than private retreat here.
If your question is belonging, this location looks thinner than it does for ambition.
```

Bad:

```text
This place could mean many different things depending on how you look at it.
There are a lot of energies here, so only you can know what it means.
This location has both supportive and challenging qualities.
```

The last example may be technically true, but it fails the service if it
never explains which qualities matter more, for what purpose, and why.

## Determinism Boundary

In this product family, "deterministic prose" means:

- absolute future-fact claims;
- guaranteed outcomes;
- universal verdicts;
- commands disguised as interpretation.

Examples to avoid:

```text
You will succeed here.
This city is where you are meant to live.
Move here.
Avoid this place.
This line guarantees love.
```

It does **not** mean:

- direct conclusions;
- interpretive commitment;
- ranking one emphasis above another;
- saying that one place is better for one function and worse for another;
- clear practical perspective.

Confidence is necessary. Absolutism is not.

The prose can be confident:

```text
This place foregrounds your public voice.
This location asks more direct participation from your Mars material.
This city makes partnership dynamics harder to keep theoretical.
```

But it must stay bounded:

```text
This is strong symbolic evidence for public emphasis here.
This does not mean the city guarantees career success.
The chart describes what becomes louder, not what life must deliver.
```

The practical standard is:

```text
State the conclusion plainly.
State the reason clearly.
State the boundary without softening the conclusion into fog.
```

## Core Metaphor

Use "foregrounding" as the central action of place.

Good locational verbs:

- foregrounds
- brings forward
- amplifies
- concentrates
- makes visible
- privatizes
- redirects
- asks for
- rewards
- pressures
- steadies
- exposes
- reorients

Avoid verbs that imply fate or guarantee:

- causes
- ensures
- promises
- protects from
- curses
- dooms
- makes you successful
- brings your soulmate
- fixes your life

## Preferred Claims

Use claim forms like:

- "This place is better suited to..."
- "This location is more supportive of... than..."
- "The stronger pattern here is..."
- "This is strong evidence for..."
- "The location appears to emphasize..."
- "The relocated chart brings..."
- "The clearest shift is..."
- "This place may reward..."
- "The pressure point is likely to be..."
- "For a short stay, this may feel like..."
- "For residence, the same signature may ask for..."
- "The evidence is strongest where..."
- "The technical reason is..."

## Prohibited Claims

Do not write:

- "This is your best place."
- "Move here to find love."
- "This city guarantees success."
- "Avoid this place."
- "You will become wealthy here."
- "This place is dangerous for you."
- "This is where you are destined to live."
- "This line means fame."
- "This location fixes your chart."

Do not confuse `prohibited` with `confident`.

These are allowed when the evidence supports them:

- "This place is better for visibility than retreat."
- "For partnership, this location looks stronger than it does for independence."
- "The chart supports a clearer conclusion around pressure than ease."
- "The convergence here is real, even though it is not a guarantee."

## Evidence Language

### Relocated Angles

Name the mechanism when it matters:

```text
With Venus near the relocated Midheaven, the public-facing part of life carries more Venusian material here: relationship, aesthetic judgment, social ease, value, and visibility.
```

Translate it into lived language:

```text
This can make the place useful for being seen through taste, collaboration, beauty, or relational intelligence.
```

Bound the claim:

```text
It does not guarantee popularity or artistic success; it marks the area of life where those questions become easier to encounter.
```

### House Shifts

Use house movement as expression, not personality change:

```text
Your natal Mercury does not become a different Mercury here. It changes rooms. What was more private or backgrounded in the birth location may become more visible through daily exchange, study, writing, or local movement.
```

### Natal Modifiers

Keep natal condition distinct from relocation:

```text
The relocated chart brings Mars forward, but Mars arrives with its natal condition. The place amplifies the function; it does not erase the original pattern.
```

### Purpose Lens

Use the user's purpose as a reading lens, not as proof:

```text
For career visibility, this evidence is useful because the relocated Midheaven is active. For rest, the same emphasis may feel too exposed unless the person has clear boundaries around public demand.
```

When a purpose lens is present, the prose should usually make a real
judgment:

```text
For career, this place has a stronger argument than it does for recovery.
For belonging, the evidence is mixed and thinner than it is for motion or reinvention.
```

## Duration Language

### Short Visit

Use:

- "may be felt as an activation"
- "can be useful for testing the signal"
- "may show up through temporary meetings, mood, or focus"
- "does not need to become a life decision"

### Extended Stay

Use:

- "repetition matters"
- "the place has time to teach its pattern"
- "the emphasis becomes less like an event and more like a climate"

### Residence

Use:

- "becomes part of the operating environment"
- "asks for sustainable participation"
- "what feels exciting at first may need structure over time"

### Past Place

Use:

- "may explain why this place carried a specific kind of charge"
- "can give language to a lived pattern"
- "does not require rewriting the memory as all good or all bad"

## Pressure Without Fear

Pressure is not punishment. Write pressure as concentration, demand, mismatch, or growth edge.

Good:

```text
This place may make the Mars pattern harder to ignore. That can be energizing when there is a clean outlet and agitating when action has nowhere honest to go.
```

Avoid:

```text
Mars here is bad for you.
```

Do not dilute real pressure into pretty ambiguity. If the evidence points
to demand, isolation, exposure, instability, or social thinning, name the
pattern directly and then bound it correctly.

Good:

```text
The place may lean toward isolation or social thinning. That does not mean
you will be lonely here, but it is a real caution if your main question is
community.
```

Avoid:

```text
This area may support personal reflection without many ripples.
```

The first version gives perspective. The second hides the point.

## Support Without Fantasy

Support is not a guarantee. Write support as availability, coherence, ease of participation, or resonance.

Good:

```text
Venus is easier to access here, especially through collaboration, design, reciprocity, and environments where value is made visible.
```

Avoid:

```text
Venus here means love and money.
```

## Technical Appendix Tone

The appendix should be clear, calm, and inspectable:

```text
Relocation was calculated from the original birth UTC instant. The destination coordinates were used only to recalculate angles and houses. Planetary longitudes remain natal.
```

For unsupported methods:

```text
Astrocartography line distance is not included in this version. This report uses relocated chart evidence only.
```

For birth-time uncertainty:

```text
Because relocated angles are time-sensitive, approximate or unknown birth times reduce confidence in angle-based claims. House-level interpretation may be omitted or marked lower confidence where needed.
```

## Section Jobs

Each section should do one job:

- **Place Signature:** name the dominant emphasis.
- **What Changes Here:** compare natal baseline to relocated expression.
- **Strongest Structural Evidence:** show the lead calculation.
- **Relocated Angles:** explain angular visibility.
- **House Shifts:** show what changes rooms.
- **Planets Brought Forward:** explain emphasized functions.
- **What This Place Rewards:** practical participation style.
- **What May Require Adjustment:** friction and overuse.
- **Duration Lens:** short stay vs residence.
- **Purpose Lens:** user's stated goal.
- **Evidence Summary:** ranked evidence in plain language.
- **Technical Appendix:** calculation truth.

## Example Claim Pattern

Use this pattern for most prose leaves:

```text
Mechanism -> lived expression -> useful participation -> boundary.
```

For synthesis leaves and high-value guidance sections, expand that to:

```text
Mechanism -> interpretation -> functional consequence -> tradeoff -> boundary.
```

Example:

```text
With Mercury brought closer to the relocated Ascendant, this place makes language, movement, questions, and quick perception part of how you meet life. It can be useful for study, writing, networking, teaching, or rebuilding your local rhythm. The gift is responsiveness; the risk is living too much through reaction. This is not a promise that every conversation matters, but it is strong evidence that your mind becomes more visible here.
```

## Anti-Flattening Rules

- Never interpret a planet without angle, house, natal condition, and purpose context.
- Never interpret a house shift without naming the kind of life arena it changes.
- Never interpret support without naming how the person participates.
- Never interpret pressure without naming its constructive use.
- Never let "good/bad" replace "fit, emphasis, tradeoff, and use."
- Never mistake bounded prose for vague prose.
- Never hide the strongest supported conclusion just to avoid sounding decisive.
