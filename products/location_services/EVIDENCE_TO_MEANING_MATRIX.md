# Location Services Evidence-To-Meaning Matrix

**Status:** First-pass content matrix for Place Resonance.  
**Scope:** How structured evidence should translate into report meaning.  
**Audience:** Content and backend leads.

## Evidence Priority

### Primary Evidence

- Planet conjunct relocated angle.
- Natal planet changes into an angular relocated house.
- Relocated Ascendant, MC, Descendant, or IC creates a major orientation shift.
- Multiple high-priority factors repeat the same planet, house, or life domain.

### Context Evidence

- Relocated house changes for personally relevant planets.
- Natal condition of emphasized planets.
- Rulership links from emphasized planets to natal houses.
- Natal aspects involving emphasized planets.
- Purpose lens fit.

### Restricted Evidence

- Astrocartography line proximity before line geometry exists.
- Parans before paran engine exists.
- Local Space before directional engine exists.
- Dynamic timing before static place baseline exists.

## Relocated Angles

### Ascendant

Core meaning:

- How the person enters the place.
- Body, pace, self-presentation, immediate response.
- What becomes part of first contact with life.

Strong prose moves:

- "This place changes how life meets you first."
- "The planet on the relocated Ascendant becomes part of your arrival pattern."
- "This is less about reputation and more about embodiment."

Pressure risk:

- Overidentifying with the activated planet.
- Feeling constantly called to perform that function.

### Midheaven

Core meaning:

- Public role, visibility, work, contribution, reputation.
- How the person becomes legible in the outer world.
- What the place asks them to take seriously.

Strong prose moves:

- "This place makes the planet harder to keep private."
- "The public-facing part of life carries this planetary material."
- "The location can reward deliberate visibility."

Pressure risk:

- Public exposure without support.
- Treating external recognition as the only proof of value.

### Descendant

Core meaning:

- Partnership, clients, mirrors, direct others, negotiation.
- What arrives through encounter.
- What the person meets across the table.

Strong prose moves:

- "This place teaches through direct contact."
- "The planet may arrive through people, agreements, conflict, attraction, or collaboration."
- "The signature is relational even when the issue is personal."

Pressure risk:

- Projection.
- Expecting others to carry the activated planet entirely.

### Imum Coeli

Core meaning:

- Home, privacy, roots, memory, belonging, inner weather.
- What the place does underneath the visible life.
- Emotional foundation and retreat pattern.

Strong prose moves:

- "This place changes the floor beneath the life."
- "The planet becomes part of home, privacy, and emotional recovery."
- "The effect may be quiet but persistent."

Pressure risk:

- Feeling enclosed by the activated material.
- Old family or belonging patterns becoming louder.

## Planetary Emphasis

### Sun

Meaning:

- Authorship, vitality, direction, confidence, central purpose.

Place expression:

- The person is asked to live with more visible self-definition.
- The place rewards coherent participation rather than passive adaptation.

Risk:

- Overidentifying with recognition or role.

### Moon

Meaning:

- Belonging, body rhythm, care, memory, emotional regulation.

Place expression:

- The place changes what feels like home, safety, appetite, and recovery.

Risk:

- Habit, nostalgia, mood, or family pattern becomes too governing.

### Mercury

Meaning:

- Language, learning, mobility, interpretation, exchange.

Place expression:

- The place rewards movement, conversation, study, writing, trade, or local networks.

Risk:

- Nervous overactivity, scattered attention, too much mediation.

### Venus

Meaning:

- Value, connection, aesthetics, pleasure, reciprocity, reception.

Place expression:

- The place brings relationship, taste, social ease, art, money choices, or self-worth forward.

Risk:

- Confusing charm with compatibility, ease with depth, or approval with value.

### Mars

Meaning:

- Action, heat, courage, boundary, desire, conflict, stamina.

Place expression:

- The place asks for cleaner action and a more honest relationship with force.

Risk:

- Irritation, burnout, urgency without aim, conflict as default.

### Jupiter

Meaning:

- Growth, opportunity, teaching, belief, scale, generosity.

Place expression:

- The place may widen access, learning, confidence, community, or horizon.

Risk:

- Overextension, optimism without proportion, saying yes too easily.

### Saturn

Meaning:

- Structure, commitment, responsibility, time, limits, maturity.

Place expression:

- The place asks for discipline, durable choices, boundaries, and adult pacing.

Risk:

- Heaviness, isolation, fear of failure, duty replacing consent.

### Uranus

Meaning:

- Breakthrough, independence, disruption, experimentation, liberation.

Place expression:

- The place may loosen old roles and make experimentation harder to avoid.

Risk:

- Instability, restlessness, change for its own sake.

### Neptune

Meaning:

- Imagination, sensitivity, spirituality, compassion, atmosphere, dissolution.

Place expression:

- The place may heighten dream, art, empathy, permeability, and longing.

Risk:

- Fog, projection, porous boundaries, idealization.

### Pluto

Meaning:

- Power, depth, compulsion, grief, transformation, truth under pressure.

Place expression:

- The place may expose buried stakes and intensify the need for honest change.

Risk:

- Control loops, obsession, isolation, treating intensity as proof.

## Natal Modifier Rules

### Dignity And Condition

Use:

```text
The place amplifies the planet, but the natal condition describes how that planet tends to operate.
```

Guidance:

- Strong condition: more reliable access, but still requires participation.
- Challenged condition: more important, not automatically worse.
- Unknown condition: state uncertainty rather than inventing nuance.

### Sect

Use sect as a support/strain modifier, not a fate label.

Guidance:

- In-sect planet brought forward: function may be easier to integrate.
- Out-of-sect planet brought forward: function may require more conscious handling.

### Natal Angularity

If a planet is already natal-angular and becomes relocated-angular:

- Treat as repeated emphasis.
- The place reinforces an already central life function.

If a planet is not natal-angular but becomes relocated-angular:

- Treat as newly foregrounded.
- The place brings a less obvious natal function into visibility.

### Rulership

If an emphasized planet rules important natal houses:

```text
The planet does not arrive alone. It carries the topics of the houses it rules.
```

Example:

```text
If relocated Venus is angular and rules natal 2nd and 7th houses, the place may tie public visibility to value, money, reciprocity, partnership, or direct agreements.
```

### Natal Aspects

Natal aspects add texture:

- Supportive aspects: the planet may have existing pathways for cooperation.
- Challenging aspects: the place may activate an existing growth edge.
- Mixed aspects: report the contradiction rather than smoothing it out.

## Purpose Lens Modifiers

### Career

Prioritize:

- MC contacts.
- 10th house shifts.
- Sun, Saturn, Jupiter, Mercury, Mars, Venus depending on field.
- Public visibility and authority language.

Avoid:

- Promising success, fame, clients, or income.

### Belonging

Prioritize:

- IC contacts.
- Moon and 4th house shifts.
- Venus and Saturn for attachment/support structures.
- Local rhythm and emotional safety language.

Avoid:

- Saying the place is "home" as a certainty.

### Rest

Prioritize:

- 4th, 12th, and Moon signatures.
- Venus, Neptune, Saturn when supportive or containing.
- Boundary and restoration language.

Avoid:

- Treating quiet signatures as automatically easy.

### Partnership

Prioritize:

- Descendant contacts.
- 7th house shifts.
- Venus, Mars, Moon, Saturn, Jupiter.
- Agreement, reciprocity, projection, and negotiation language.

Avoid:

- Love guarantees.

### Creative Visibility

Prioritize:

- 5th and 10th house shifts.
- Venus, Sun, Mercury, Neptune, Mars.
- Audience, art, pleasure, presentation, practice language.

Avoid:

- Fame or artistic destiny claims.

### Study

Prioritize:

- 3rd and 9th house shifts.
- Mercury, Jupiter, Saturn.
- Learning, publishing, mentorship, travel, worldview language.

Avoid:

- Certifying external achievement.

### Retreat

Prioritize:

- IC, 12th house, Moon, Neptune, Saturn.
- Privacy, recovery, dream, grief, completion, spiritual maintenance.

Avoid:

- Dissolving practical reality into mysticism.

## Evidence Conflict Handling

Do not force every signal into agreement.

Example:

```text
The relocated MC suggests public visibility, while the relocated IC/Moon signature asks for privacy and recovery. This does not make the place wrong. It means the location may ask you to build a more honest boundary between public contribution and private restoration.
```

Conflict types:

- Visibility vs privacy.
- Expansion vs structure.
- Relationship vs independence.
- Action vs rest.
- Beauty/ease vs depth/intensity.

## Minimum Evidence For A Strong Claim

Use strong language when at least one is true:

- A personal planet is tightly conjunct a relocated angle.
- A planet enters an angular relocated house and has natal relevance.
- Multiple evidence types repeat the same planet/domain.
- Purpose lens matches the strongest relocated evidence.

Use softer language when:

- Evidence is house-only.
- Birth time is approximate.
- Multiple signals conflict.
- The backend only provides scaffold fields.

## Unsupported Method Language

If the method is not computed:

```text
This version does not include astrocartography line-distance claims. The reading is based on relocated chart evidence only.
```

If future method is planned:

```text
Astrocartography can later add map-line evidence, but it should not be inferred from this relocated chart alone.
```
