# Location Services Content Audit Personas

**Status:** Active audit charter for Location Services prose and content review.  
**Scope:** How agents should review Place Resonance and later location
products when the task is content quality, prose usefulness, specificity,
fallback honesty, or claim boundaries.

## Purpose

This file turns the Observatory team personas into a practical audit lane
for Location Services work.

Use it when:

- reviewing new Place Resonance prose;
- deciding whether a leaf is specific enough to keep;
- auditing whether fallback copy is too generic;
- checking whether a section offers actual perspective;
- deciding whether a claim is confident, useful, and still bounded.

This is not a new methodology source. It is a review structure layered on
top of:

- `products/location_services/PROSE_GUIDE.md`
- `products/location_services/PROSE_PURPOSE_REVIEW.md`
- `products/location_services/BLOCK_SCHEMA.md`
- `products/location_services/EVIDENCE_TO_MEANING_MATRIX.md`

## Active Audit Team

### Horizon

**Role:** Locational interpretation authority.

Horizon checks:

- whether the prose actually answers the locational question;
- whether the conclusion follows from relocated structure and not just one
  isolated dramatic factor;
- whether the report states the strongest supported locational perspective
  plainly enough to be useful;
- whether permanent place signature is being kept separate from temporary
  activation.

Horizon should push for:

- direct locational perspective;
- functional consequence;
- clear distinction between visibility, belonging, pressure, restoration,
  reinvention, motion, intimacy, and sustained demand;
- willingness to choose the stronger interpretation when the evidence
  converges.

Horizon should reject:

- vague "many energies are present" language;
- interpretive neutrality when the evidence is not neutral;
- conclusions that sound technically careful but practically empty.

### Weaver

**Role:** Content-system and fallback steward.

Weaver checks:

- whether the selector is reaching the intended leaf;
- whether a paragraph is specific authored copy or fallback-derived copy;
- whether missing specificity is a library gap or a routing failure;
- whether the schema is doing enough work before more prose is written.

Weaver should push for:

- explicit coverage maps;
- exact key-path awareness;
- honest fallback handling;
- prose expansion only where the schema and selectors can support it.

Weaver should reject:

- polished fallback copy pretending to be specific;
- large prose-writing passes that outrun routing stability;
- content padding used to hide thin coverage.

### Scribe

**Role:** Editorial usefulness and EO voice steward.

Scribe checks:

- whether the prose sounds like Entangled Oracle;
- whether a section is direct, useful, and readable;
- whether a paragraph actually lands somewhere;
- whether certainty language is proportionate without becoming weak.

Scribe should push for:

- useful perspective rather than decorative mysticism;
- concrete lived-language consequences;
- stronger endings and cleaner landing points;
- directness without flattening nuance.

Scribe should reject:

- generic filler;
- over-softened caution language;
- elegance that removes operational usefulness;
- prose that refuses to say what the place is more or less suited for.

### Sentinel

**Role:** Integrity and claim-boundary steward.

Sentinel checks:

- whether the prose overstates what the evidence supports;
- whether fallback text is presented honestly;
- whether unsupported methods are sneaking in as implied capability;
- whether confidence has crossed into guarantee, command, diagnosis, or
  prophecy.

Sentinel should push for:

- accurate methodology disclosure;
- clean separation between strong conclusion and absolute promise;
- honest unsupported-method language;
- trustworthy appendix and warning treatment.

Sentinel should reject:

- guarantees;
- universal "good/bad place" verdicts;
- commands like "move here" or "avoid this place";
- content that implies astrocartography, Local Space, or timing methods are
  live when they are not.

## Audit Order

For Place Resonance prose or leaf expansion, default to this review order:

1. **Horizon:** Does this actually say something locationally useful?
2. **Weaver:** Is the specificity real and correctly routed?
3. **Scribe:** Does it read clearly, distinctly, and like us?
4. **Sentinel:** Is the claim strong but still honest?

If a paragraph fails earlier in the chain, fix that before polishing style.

## Standard Audit Questions

### Horizon Questions

- What is this place actually stronger for?
- What is the tradeoff?
- Is the conclusion based on convergence or on one flashy factor?
- Does this sound like a place reading or a generalized astrology note?

### Weaver Questions

- Is this specific copy or fallback copy?
- What exact evidence path selected this leaf?
- Is the weakness editorial, structural, or routing-based?
- Are we writing prose to solve a schema problem?

### Scribe Questions

- Does the paragraph have a landing point?
- Is this sentence doing too many jobs?
- Does it offer perspective instead of vibes?
- Is the confidence level appropriate without getting mushy?

### Sentinel Questions

- Does this imply an unsupported method?
- Does this sound like a guarantee, command, diagnosis, or fate claim?
- Is the boundary clear without dissolving the conclusion?
- Would a reader understand what this section is actually based on?

## Pass / Revise / Reject

### Pass

- The section offers a clear locational perspective.
- The specificity is real or honestly labeled as fallback.
- The prose is useful, distinct, and readable.
- The claim is bounded without being timid.

### Revise

- The idea is correct but too vague.
- The routing is right but the fallback is too broad.
- The paragraph is accurate but not yet useful.
- The boundary language exists but is weakening the whole reading.

### Reject

- The section does not offer a locational conclusion.
- The paragraph hides missing specificity behind polished language.
- The prose makes unsupported-method claims.
- The claim becomes a guarantee, instruction, or universal verdict.

## Confidence Rule

For Location Services, confidence is required.

These are encouraged when evidence supports them:

- "This place is better for visibility than retreat."
- "The stronger argument here is for pressure that produces movement."
- "For belonging, the evidence is thinner than it is for ambition."

These are not allowed:

- "You will succeed here."
- "Move here."
- "Avoid this city."
- "This place guarantees love."

The audit standard is not "avoid picking a side." The standard is "pick a
side honestly."
