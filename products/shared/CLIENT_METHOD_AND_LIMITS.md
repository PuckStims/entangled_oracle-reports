# Client-Facing Methodology and Limits

## Standard Methodology Language

These reports use the Tropical zodiac and Whole Sign houses.

The Tropical zodiac measures the chart against the seasonal zodiac used in much of contemporary Western astrology. Whole Sign houses treat the full sign rising at birth as the first house and then assign the remaining houses sign by sign around the chart.

## Interpretive Nature

This report is symbolic and interpretive rather than deterministic. It is designed to describe themes, patterns, pressures, and timing windows that may be useful for reflection, planning, and conversation. It does not guarantee a fixed event or outcome.

## Birth-Time Language

When an exact birth time is available, house and angle-based statements can be used more directly.

When birth time is unknown or approximate, time-sensitive material is reduced, softened, or withheld rather than presented as exact.

## Layer Distinction Language

Standard astrology refers to the chart foundation: luminaries, planets, houses, aspects, rulership, and other core chart structures.

Established-niche material refers to specialist bodies or methods that remain distinct from the standard chart foundation.

EO material is an additional proprietary layer. It does not replace the standard chart foundation. It extends or refracts patterns that have already been established through the standard chart.

## Forecast Timing Language

Forecast timing describes periods of emphasis, activation, or review. It does not predict a single unavoidable external event. A timing window is best treated as a period when certain themes are more available, more visible, or more likely to require attention.

## Tone Guardrails

Client-facing language should remain:

- clear
- proportionate
- non-deterministic
- non-diagnostic
- non-moralizing
- grounded in the active methodology
