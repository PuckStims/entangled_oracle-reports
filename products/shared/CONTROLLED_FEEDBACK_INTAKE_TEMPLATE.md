## Review Areas

### Calculation clarity

- What felt clear?
- What felt unclear?

### Report readability

- What was easy to follow?
- Where did pacing or density become difficult?

### Visual design

- What supported comprehension?
- What distracted from comprehension?

### Usefulness

- Which sections felt most usable?
- Which sections felt less useful?

### Tone

- Where did the tone feel well-calibrated?
- Where did it feel too abstract, too technical, or too strong?

### Confusion points

- Which terms, sections, or transitions caused confusion?

### Technical errors

- Any factual, routing, formatting, or rendering errors observed?

### Requested expansions

- What would you want more of?
- What should remain out of scope?

## Internal Handling Rule

Feedback is collected for review. It does not automatically become a product requirement. Any requested change must be checked against the completed Tropical + Whole Sign architecture before being accepted into the production baseline.
