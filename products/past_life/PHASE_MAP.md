# Past Life Phase Map

**Status:** Delivery and handoff map.  
**Purpose:** Break the Past Life line into build phases that can be delegated efficiently, with Gemini handling high-volume construction and Codex handling bounded audit, cleanup, and seam-specific correction.

## Delivery Strategy

Use the following division of labor:

- `Gemini`: power through first-pass drafting, inventory work, broad block authoring, and repetitive structural expansion
- `Codex`: audit active seams, tighten scope, correct structural mistakes, clean prose drift, verify runtime truth, and patch only the bounded seam

This product line is a good fit for that split because:

- the destination can be specified clearly in advance
- a lot of the work will be high-volume authored block generation
- the dangerous part is not the first draft volume, but drift in method, product boundaries, and runtime wiring

## Fixed Destination

The destination is not "some karmic notes."

The destination is:

1. a Soul Ecosystem-integrated Past Life layer
2. a source-grounded method stack led by Steven Forrest
3. a bounded proprietary-formula expansion lane for retroactive karmic analysis
4. a future-ready standalone Past Life product path

Sequencing can be staged.
The destination should not be shrunk.

## Stop Conditions

Use these to avoid endless polishing loops.

- stop a block-authoring pass once the major method contract is being honored
- stop a cleanup pass once remaining issues are stylistic rather than structural
- regenerate a family instead of hand-editing it forever if the draft is wrong at the method level
- do not widen a Codex cleanup pass into a full rewrite unless the user explicitly asks
