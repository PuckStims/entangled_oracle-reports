# IP Risk Notes - Nonlegal Draft

This is a strategic product note, not legal advice. Before commercial release at scale, consult an IP attorney.

## General principle

U.S. copyright law does not protect ideas, systems, methods of operation, procedures, concepts, principles, or discoveries. It protects original expression. This supports the strategy of building a distinct system from public-domain or broadly available symbolic ingredients while avoiding copied expression.

## Practical risk map

### Lower risk

- Building a new synthesis from astrology, tarot, polarity-inspired current language, historical esoteric concepts, and original EO mechanics.
- Using original names, diagrams, report structure, scoring logic, and interpretive text.
- Describing EIA as proprietary to Entangled Oracle.
- Using broad concepts like decision-making, energy rhythm, restoration, boundaries, symbolic archetypes, and field sensitivity.

### Higher risk

- Using Human Design names, diagrams, or branded phrases.
- Using BodyGraph-like visuals.
- Copying report wording, chart graphics, teaching language, course structure, or branded categories.
- Implying official Human Design affiliation or certification.
- Using confusingly similar product names.

## EIA protection strategy

1. Use independent source basis.
2. Keep visible object model distinct.
3. Keep visuals distinct.
4. Keep consumer promise practical and original.
5. Maintain source notes and design-history documentation.
6. Avoid HD trademarks and proprietary vocabulary.
7. Build original interpretive copy from EO content packs.

## EIA defensibility notes

EIA's object model is:

```text
Registers -> Modes -> Mechanism -> Distortion -> Restoration -> Experiment
```

Human Design's public object model is commonly presented through:

```text
BodyGraph -> Type -> Strategy -> Authority -> Profile -> Centers -> Gates/Channels
```

This should remain the central differentiation.

## Trademark caution

Even when concepts are not copyrightable, names and branding may raise trademark concerns. Do not use Human Design as a product label except in internal research or comparative notes. Public marketing should not imply endorsement or affiliation.

## Copy caution

Do not use AI to paraphrase Human Design text into EIA. Draft EIA from its own source model and content pack.
