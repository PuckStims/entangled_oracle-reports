## Tone guide

EIA voice should be:

- precise but not clinical,
- mystical but not gullible,
- practical but not flattened,
- validating but not indulgent,
- invitational but not passive,
- structured but not deterministic.

## Consumer-facing disclaimer

> EIA is a symbolic reflection system. It is designed to support discernment, self-observation, and practical experimentation. It does not diagnose, predict with certainty, or replace real-world information, professional advice, consent, or lived context.
