# Do Not Copy From Human Design

## Purpose

This file protects EIA from becoming a thinly re-skinned Human Design derivative.

## Do not use these visible objects

- Human Design System
- BodyGraph
- Type
- Strategy
- Authority
- Profile
- Incarnation Cross
- Centers
- Defined / undefined centers
- Gates
- Channels
- Lines, if used in HD-specific form
- Aura mechanics
- Not-self doctrine
- Deconditioning doctrine
- Rave terminology
- Mandala/BodyGraph visual arrangement
- HD chart diagram style

## Do not imitate the object model

Avoid:

```text
Person -> Type -> Strategy -> Authority -> Profile -> Centers -> Gates/Channels
```

Use:

```text
Person -> Registers -> Modes -> Mechanisms -> Distortions -> Restorations -> Experiments
```

## Do not imitate the visual grammar

Avoid:

- human silhouette with centers connected by channels,
- colored/white centers,
- hexagram/gate mandala around a bodygraph,
- chart tables labeled like Human Design reports.

Use:

- circuit-mandala hybrid,
- concentric architecture rings,
- seven-register dashboard,
- altar-cloth/grid instrument panel,
- polarity compass,
- signal-flow map.

## Do not imitate the promise

Avoid:

- "your genetic blueprint",
- "your correct strategy",
- "your authority tells you",
- "live your design",
- "not-self" framing.

Use:

- "your signal path",
- "your decision tempo",
- "your restoration key",
- "your energy economy",
- "your boundary interface",
- "try this experiment and observe what changes."

## Acceptable inspiration level

EIA may share the broad category of "birth-data-based symbolic operating map" with Human Design, just as many systems share astrology, tarot, Kabbalah, I Ching, chakra symbolism, or energy-body language.

EIA should not share Human Design's proprietary expression, diagram, terminology, or report architecture.
