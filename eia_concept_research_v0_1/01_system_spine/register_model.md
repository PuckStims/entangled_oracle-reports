# EIA Register Model

## 1. Ignition Register

**Question:** How does movement begin?

Modes:

- **Direct Spark** - action begins through immediate ignition, desire, pressure, or courage.
- **Responsive Pull** - action begins when something in the field gives traction.
- **Structured Commitment** - action begins once responsibility, form, or obligation is clear.
- **Relational Opening** - action begins through contact, invitation, mirrored need, or agreement.
- **Threshold Break** - action begins when pressure makes the old pattern impossible.
- **Quiet Accumulation** - action begins after private preparation reaches enough density.

Primary astrology candidates:

- Sun, Mars, Ascendant, chart ruler.
- 1st house, Aries, fire signs, cardinal modality.
- Mars aspects to Sun, Moon, ASC, MC.
- Saturn/Mars combinations for structured commitment.
- Uranus/Pluto aspects for threshold break.

## 2. Reception Register

**Question:** How does signal enter the system?

Modes:

- **Open Field** - receives broadly; benefits from filtering and grounding.
- **Selective Gate** - receives clearly when access is chosen and limited.
- **Mirror System** - reflects the room and needs separation from over-identification.
- **Deep Sponge** - absorbs atmosphere, mood, grief, longing, and subtext.
- **Pattern Receiver** - notices repeating structures over time.
- **Low-Noise Receiver** - needs quiet, evidence, and reduced input to perceive accurately.

Primary astrology candidates:

- Moon, Mercury, Neptune, 4th/8th/12th houses.
- Water emphasis, mutable emphasis.
- Moon-Neptune, Mercury-Neptune, Mercury-Uranus, Saturn-Mercury aspects.
- 12th house placements.

## 3. Decision Register

**Question:** How does clarity become trustworthy?

Modes:

- **Immediate Knowing** - clarity arrives fast; needs review for nuance.
- **Tidal Knowing** - clarity stabilizes across emotional cycles.
- **Embodied Check** - the body confirms or resists before the mind fully justifies.
- **Dialogic Knowing** - clarity sharpens through safe conversation.
- **Pattern Recognition** - clarity arrives by comparing signals over time.
- **Stillness First** - clarity appears after noise, urgency, and audience are removed.

Primary astrology candidates:

- Moon condition for tidal knowing.
- Mercury condition for dialogic/pattern recognition.
- Venus/Mars/Sun embodiment for body-check modes.
- Saturn for time-tested clarity.
- Neptune and 12th house for stillness/noise sensitivity.

## 4. Current Register

**Question:** How does energy move, spend, recover, and circulate?

Modes:

- **Steady Flame** - reliable when maintained; depleted by over-obligation.
- **Pulse Current** - works in surges and recoveries.
- **Deep Reservoir** - slow to access, powerful once engaged.
- **Borrowed Charge** - amplifies around people/contexts and needs decompression.
- **Seasonal Field** - thrives through cycles, not sameness.
- **Precision Battery** - energy increases when the task is specific and meaningful.

Primary astrology candidates:

- Sun/Mars/Jupiter/Saturn conditions.
- Fire for flame/pulse.
- Earth/Saturn for steady and precision modes.
- Water/Pluto/8th/12th for deep reservoir.
- 7th/11th/Moon/Venus emphasis for borrowed charge.

Polarity mapping:

- Positive current: outward, initiating, radiating, activating.
- Negative current: receiving, drawing inward, magnetizing, absorbing.
- Neutral current: integrating, regulating, witnessing, stabilizing.

## 5. Boundary Register

**Question:** How does selfhood hold shape under contact, demand, intimacy, and pressure?

Modes:

- **Membrane** - permeable but capable of filtering.
- **Gate** - opens selectively; thrives with consent and explicit agreements.
- **Mirror** - reflects others, requiring identity anchors.
- **Wall** - protects strongly; risks isolation.
- **Anchor** - stabilizes others; risks becoming everyone's floor.
- **Blade** - cuts distortion quickly; needs gentleness after severance.
- **Vessel** - contains complexity; risks over-holding what should move.

Primary astrology candidates:

- ASC/DSC, 1st/7th axis.
- Saturn, Lilith, Mars, Pluto, Chiron.
- Air houses for agreement/contract boundaries.
- Water houses for emotional containment.

## 6. Contact Register

**Question:** What happens when this person enters relationship, group fields, audience, or intimacy?

Modes:

- **Catalyst** - accelerates truth, change, and confrontation.
- **Stabilizer** - helps others regulate and organize.
- **Translator** - makes the unsaid legible.
- **Amplifier** - increases emotional or symbolic volume.
- **Witness** - sees clearly without needing to intervene immediately.
- **Sanctuary** - creates space where others soften and reveal.
- **Challenger** - presses against false peace or unstable agreements.

Primary astrology candidates:

- 7th, 10th, 11th houses.
- Venus, Moon, Mercury, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Pluto.
- EAS Catalyst, Narrative Current, Impact Radius.
- MC and public/vocational structure.

## 7. Restoration Register

**Question:** How does coherence return?

Modes:

- **Solitude and Sleep** - restoration through withdrawal, rest, low input.
- **Movement and Heat** - restoration through action, sweat, rhythm, breath.
- **Beauty and Pleasure** - restoration through aesthetic nourishment, sensuality, value.
- **Naming and Writing** - restoration through language, journaling, recording, categorizing.
- **Ritual and Repetition** - restoration through repeated symbolic structure.
- **Trusted Witnessing** - restoration through safe reflection with another.
- **Material Simplification** - restoration through cleaning, budgeting, reducing friction.
- **Clean Agreement** - restoration through renegotiation, boundary, explicit commitment.

Primary astrology candidates:

- Moon, Venus, Mercury, Saturn, Neptune.
- 4th/6th/12th houses.
- Earth emphasis for material simplification.
- Air/Mercury for naming/writing.
- Venus/Moon for beauty/pleasure.
- Saturn for ritual/repetition.
