# Sample Register Output

## Decision Register: Tidal Knowing + Immediate Recognition

### Mode

**Tidal Knowing** with **Immediate Recognition** as a secondary signal.

### Mechanism

Your first recognition may arrive quickly, but the decision becomes trustworthy when it remains true across more than one emotional weather pattern. The first yes or no is useful data. It is not always the whole answer.

### Distortion

Urgency can impersonate certainty. When pressured to answer before the feeling system has completed its pass, you may agree from momentum and discover your actual truth after the heat fades.

### Restoration

Return to the question after the emotional state changes. If the answer stays clear across heat, quiet, irritation, tenderness, and rest, it is more likely to be structurally true.

### Experiment

For seven days, mark decisions as **spark**, **tide**, or **settled**. Do not judge the first answer. Watch which choices remain coherent after one sleep cycle.

## Boundary Register: Selective Gate

### Mode

**Selective Gate**

### Mechanism

Your system does not need to become less connected. It needs chosen access. Agreements, timing, and explicit limits make your warmth safer to inhabit.

### Distortion

When boundaries are vague, obligation can enter disguised as love, loyalty, urgency, or usefulness. You may begin managing the emotional room before deciding whether you consented to that role.

### Restoration

Name what is actually available. A clean agreement protects dignity without making connection the enemy.

### Experiment

Before saying yes, answer: **What am I offering, for how long, and what would make this too expensive?**

## Current Register: Pulse Current

### Mode

**Pulse Current**

### Mechanism

Your energy may arrive in waves rather than an even daily stream. When the pulse is alive, progress can be fast and vivid. When the pulse recedes, the system is not failing; it is metabolizing.

### Distortion

You may treat the high-current version of yourself as the only legitimate version, then punish the recovery phase as laziness or inconsistency.

### Restoration

Build containers that expect surges and returns: batch work, recovery blocks, flexible deadlines, and rituals that close the loop before depletion becomes collapse.

### Experiment

Track energy in three labels only: **charging**, **active**, **integrating**. Plan from the pattern instead of demanding sameness.
