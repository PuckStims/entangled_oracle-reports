# Odic Field Sensitivity Mapping

## Research basis

Odic force, or odyle/od, was a 19th-century hypothetical vital force proposed by Baron Karl von Reichenbach. Historical descriptions associate it with crystals, magnets, the human body, heat, chemical action, electricity, and the perception of "sensitives."

## EIA use

Do not claim odic force is measurable or scientifically established. Use it as a historical-esoteric ancestor for the idea of **field sensitivity**.

## EIA field-sensitivity layer

This layer asks:

- How much does atmosphere affect the person's clarity?
- Does place change the person's signal quality?
- Does relational pressure register as body/state change?
- Does symbolic material become meaningful quickly?
- Does the person need low-input environments to know what is true?

## Possible modes

### Low Field Noise

Needs clear evidence, direct signal, and low symbolic inflation.

### Atmospheric Receiver

Registers mood, space, tone, and environmental shifts quickly.

### Charge Amplifier

Intensifies what enters the field; benefits from deliberate grounding.

### Environmental Barometer

Place and context strongly affect clarity, energy, and coherence.

### Symbolic Sensor

Meaning arrives through images, omens, dreams, metaphor, coincidence, and pattern.

### Magnetized Threshold

Certain places, people, or moments feel disproportionately activating; the task is discernment, not blind obedience.

## Placement in EIA

Field Sensitivity should not be one of the seven main registers in v0.1. Instead, it can be:

- an optional modifier inside Reception Register,
- an optional State Snapshot paragraph,
- a visual overlay on the Architecture Map,
- a future premium report add-on.

## Report-safe language

Use:

> This layer uses historical odic-force language as an esoteric metaphor for field sensitivity. It does not claim a measurable force or medical effect.

Avoid:

- "You emit odic force."
- "Your odic field measures high."
- "Crystals/magnets prove your reading."
