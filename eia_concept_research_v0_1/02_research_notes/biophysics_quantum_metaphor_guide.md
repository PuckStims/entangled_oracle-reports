# Biophysics + Quantum Metaphor Guide

## Biophysics use

Biophysics is a real scientific field using physics, chemistry, math, engineering, and related disciplines to understand living systems. EIA should not pretend natal astrology is biophysics. Instead, use biophysical language carefully as grounding metaphor.

## EIA-safe biophysical language

Use:

- signal
- noise
- threshold
- load
- capacity
- coherence
- recovery curve
- regulation
- input/output
- feedback loop
- system state
- saturation
- integration

Avoid:

- "This chart proves your nervous system..."
- "Quantum biology shows your tarot pull..."
- "Your cells vibrate at..."
- "Scientifically measured energy blueprint."

## Quantum metaphor use

Quantum mechanics can inspire metaphors around state, probability, observation, measurement, and contextual outcomes. But EIA should not claim divination is literally quantum physics.

## EIA state language

The metaphor:

```text
Baseline architecture = stable state-space tendencies.
Entropy-derived reading = measurement event / sampled state.
Current transits = timing pressure.
User check-in = context constraint.
Report output = interpretation of the currently observed pattern.
```

## Report-safe wording

Use:

> The State Snapshot borrows the language of measurement as metaphor: it samples what is active now, rather than redefining who you permanently are.

Use:

> The entropy seed creates a reproducible symbolic draw. The meaning comes from the interpretive system, not from a claim that quantum mechanics causes the cards.

Avoid:

- "Tarot collapses your quantum wavefunction."
- "The universe selects this card through quantum entanglement."
- "This is scientifically proven quantum divination."

## Useful distinction

**Literal implementation:** entropy source -> cryptographic seed -> deterministic draw.  
**Metaphorical interpretation:** measurement event -> state snapshot -> reflective overlay.

This distinction is central to EIA's credibility.
