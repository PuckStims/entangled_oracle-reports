# Polarity Therapy Mapping

## Research basis

Polarity Therapy is associated with Dr. Randolph Stone. Public Polarity Therapy sources describe it in terms of balancing energy flow, positive/negative/neutral forces, and five-element frameworks.

## EIA use

EIA should not use Polarity Therapy as a medical claim, diagnosis, or bodywork instruction. It should use a polarity-inspired current model as symbolic architecture.

## EIA current model

### Positive Current

EIA language:

- output
- initiation
- radiance
- projection
- movement toward
- activation
- expressive heat

Possible register effects:

- Ignition: Direct Spark
- Current: Steady Flame / Pulse Current
- Contact: Catalyst / Challenger / Amplifier

### Negative Current

EIA language:

- reception
- absorption
- magnetism
- inward pull
- containment
- gestation
- restoration through withdrawal

Possible register effects:

- Reception: Open Field / Deep Sponge
- Current: Deep Reservoir / Borrowed Charge
- Boundary: Membrane / Vessel

### Neutral Current

EIA language:

- integration
- regulation
- witnessing
- pause
- balance
- synthesis
- still point

Possible register effects:

- Decision: Stillness First / Pattern Recognition
- Contact: Witness / Stabilizer
- Restoration: Ritual and Repetition / Solitude and Sleep

## Report-safe language

Use:

- "This report uses polarity as a symbolic current model."
- "Positive, negative, and neutral describe movement tendencies, not moral value."
- "This is not a health assessment or energy diagnosis."

Avoid:

- "Your energy is medically blocked."
- "This proves your body has X imbalance."
- "This replaces care from a qualified practitioner."
