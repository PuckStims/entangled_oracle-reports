# Human Design Boundary Map

## Why this matters

Human Design proves consumer demand for a birth-data-driven symbolic operating manual. EIA should satisfy that desire while avoiding Human Design's protected branding, language, diagrams, and surface structure.

## Publicly visible HD spine, for differentiation

Human Design public materials describe a chart calculated from birth data and incorporating I Ching, astrology, Kabbalah, the Hindu-Brahman chakra system, and quantum physics. Public-facing HD components include BodyGraph, Type, Strategy, Authority, Profile, Definition, Centers, Channels, and Gates.

## EIA differentiation spine

| Human Design object | EIA replacement | Difference |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Register signature | Multiple functional axes, not one class |
| Strategy | Ignition + Contact patterns | How movement/contact begins, not obedience rule |
| Authority | Decision Tempo | How clarity stabilizes, not fixed authority doctrine |
| Centers | Registers | Operational functions, no bodygraph centers |
| Gates | Modes / source leaves | Interpretive outputs, no I Ching gate mapping |
| Channels | Register interactions | Signal-flow relationships, no bodygraph circuitry |
| Profile | Architecture summary | Contextual operating shape, not HD line pair |
| Incarnation Cross | World Expression | Directional theme, not HD cross vocabulary |
| BodyGraph | Architecture Map | Circuit-mandala / dashboard / compass visuals |

## EIA safety rule

The goal is not to hide influence. The goal is to build a separate system with a separate source basis.

EIA should be able to answer:

- What does it calculate that HD does not calculate?
- What does it display that HD does not display?
- What does it call things that HD does not call them?
- What visual structure proves this is not a BodyGraph cousin?
- What practical use does it offer beyond type identification?

## Core consumer contrast

Human Design often feels like:

> Here is what you are and how to live your design.

EIA should feel like:

> Here is how your signal moves, where it distorts, and what restores coherence.
