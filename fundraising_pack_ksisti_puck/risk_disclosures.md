# Risk Disclosures And Boundaries

Use these boundaries in campaign pages, investor conversations, and product pages.

## General Portfolio Risks

- The portfolio is early-stage.
- Several projects are prototypes or scaffolds, not finished commercial products.
- Current revenue, if any, is not established in this pack.
- Product-market fit is unproven.
- The founder's current hardware/workspace constraints affect development velocity.
- Multiple products create focus risk.
- Production deployment, QA, CI/CD, auth, billing, and customer support are incomplete or uneven across products.

## Crowdfunding Boundary

Reward or donation crowdfunding should not promise:

- equity;
- repayment;
- revenue share;
- profit participation;
- investment upside;
- guaranteed access to future paid products;
- medical, clinical, or financial outcomes.

Supporters are helping fund operating capacity and product refinement.

## Investor Boundary

Any sale of equity, debt, SAFE notes, revenue share, tokenized interest, or profit participation may be a securities offering.

In the United States, Regulation Crowdfunding offerings generally require formal issuer disclosures and use of a registered funding portal or broker-dealer. Private offerings may require a different exemption and may limit who can invest.

This pack is not legal advice.

## Entangled Oracle Boundaries

- Astrology and symbolic products should not be presented as scientific certainty.
- Predictive claims should be cautious and clearly framed.
- Implemented engine/report features should be distinguished from planned location, relationship, or advanced timing features.
- AI synthesis should not invent chart data, placements, transits, cards, runes, or source facts.

## Shadow Compass Boundaries

- Shadow Compass is not therapy, medical care, diagnosis, crisis support, or a replacement for licensed professionals.
- Pattern language should remain user-confirmed or hypothesis-based.
- High-activation states should route to regulation and grounding before interpretation.
- Private journal/check-in text should not be treated as ordinary analytics data.

## ChartTrace ScribeShield Boundaries

- ScribeShield does not generate clinical notes.
- ScribeShield does not diagnose or recommend treatment.
- v0.1 uses deterministic synthetic cases and seeded findings.
- Real clinical deployment would require privacy, security, validation, and workflow review.
- It should be framed as documentation-integrity support, not clinician replacement.

## FlareFrame MG Signal Boundaries

- MG Signal is a personal information organizer and appointment-prep tool.
- It does not diagnose, treat, prevent, predict, or recommend medical care.
- Missing data means not captured, not normal or negative.
- Reported positive feedback from disabled individuals is useful usability signal, not clinical validation.
- Before broader release, accessibility, privacy, export, backup, and medical-claims review should be completed.

## Suggested Campaign Disclaimer

Ksisti-Puck LLC's starter platforms are early software projects. This campaign funds operational capacity: hardware, workspace, software subscriptions, documentation, testing, and product refinement. It does not offer equity, debt, profit participation, or financial return. Health, wellness, clinical, astrology, and symbolic tools are bounded carefully and should not be treated as substitutes for professional care, clinical judgment, emergency support, or scientific certainty.
