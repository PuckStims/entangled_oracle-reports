# Ksisti-Puck LLC Starter Platform Portfolio

## Investor Memo Draft

This memo is for early investor, advisor, or strategic partner conversations. It should not be treated as a securities offering document. Formal investment fundraising requires legal review and the appropriate exemption, portal, or private offering process.

## Executive Summary

Ksisti-Puck LLC is an independent software studio with four starter platform lines:

1. **Entangled Oracle** - astrology, symbolic systems, report generation, mobile, and dashboard tooling.
2. **Shadow Compass** - guided reflection, regulation, consent-aware pattern review, and cautious AI workflow architecture.
3. **ChartTrace ScribeShield** - documentation-integrity review for AI-generated clinical notes.
4. **FlareFrame MG Signal** - Myasthenia Gravis-focused local-first health logging and appointment-summary tooling.

The portfolio is pre-revenue or minimally commercialized. The primary asset is implemented software, domain-specific product architecture, authored content/data structures, and a clear pattern of cautious source-bound design.

The immediate operational bottleneck is development infrastructure: hardware, workspace, and recurring software/tooling subscriptions.

## Market Thesis

The shared thesis is that users increasingly need software that combines:

- personal meaning-making;
- source traceability;
- privacy/consent boundaries;
- cautious AI support;
- human-review workflows;
- domain-specific structured reports.

Ksisti-Puck LLC's current advantage is not scale. It is domain-specific product imagination already translated into working starter code.

## Product Lines

### Entangled Oracle

Entangled Oracle is the deepest technical and content asset in the portfolio. It combines astrology calculation, report generation, authored interpretation blocks, symbolic systems, mobile/front-end companions, and provenance posture.

Near-term commercial path:

- paid generated reports;
- local-first Android companion;
- symbolic dashboard;
- specialist astrology/report beta;
- content-pack and subscription expansion.

Primary risk:

- crowded astrology market, production polish gap, and need to avoid unsupported predictive claims.

### Shadow Compass

Shadow Compass is a consent-aware emotional reflection platform. It is architected around check-ins, state shift, guided sessions, journals, pattern review, and report generation.

Near-term commercial path:

- guided self-reflection subscription;
- practitioner-created journey tools;
- privacy-first journaling/pattern review;
- non-clinical coaching/wellness positioning.

Primary risk:

- crowded wellness/AI journaling market and need to avoid therapy replacement claims.

### ChartTrace ScribeShield

ScribeShield is the most narrowly positioned B2B-style product. It reviews generated clinical notes against source packets and flags unsupported claims, contradictions, omissions, attribution errors, overstatement, and boundary concerns.

Near-term commercial path:

- synthetic validation demo;
- clinician/supervisor discovery;
- AI scribe QA add-on;
- compliance/audit workflow prototype;
- pilot with synthetic or de-identified data.

Primary risk:

- real NLP/evidence mapping is not yet implemented; clinical and compliance markets require trust, security, and validation.

### FlareFrame MG Signal

MG Signal is a Myasthenia Gravis-focused local-first Android companion for logging flares, symptoms, treatments, recovery, function, and appointment summaries. Early micro-feedback from disabled individuals is reportedly positive, but this should be treated as qualitative usability signal rather than clinical validation.

Near-term commercial path:

- patient-focused Android app;
- doctor visit mode;
- export/PDF summaries;
- condition-pack expansion;
- disability-community beta testing.

Primary risk:

- health claims, accessibility needs, privacy, and medical-device/regulatory boundaries.

## Investment Framing

This is not a mature SaaS investment today. It is an early IP/studio capacity investment.

The investable premise is:

- multiple real starter systems already exist;
- the founder can translate specialized domain knowledge into software;
- modest operating capital can materially improve development throughput;
- a narrow product focus could convert current IP into customer-facing demos and pilots.

The conservative investment concern is:

- portfolio sprawl;
- lack of revenue;
- production-readiness gaps;
- health/clinical compliance needs;
- founder hardware/workspace constraints;
- uncertain go-to-market.
