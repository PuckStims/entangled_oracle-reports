# Ksisti-Puck LLC Starter Platform Fundraising Pack

Prepared as a practical starter pack for non-dilutive crowdfunding, early supporter outreach, and cautious investor conversations.

This pack covers four current starter platforms:

- Entangled Oracle + Entangled Astrology / Dashboard
- Shadow Compass
- ChartTrace ScribeShield
- FlareFrame MG Signal

## Recommended Use

Use the public crowdfunding materials for supporters, early users, friends, patrons, disability/community networks, and people who want to help improve the operating environment.

Use the investor memo only for serious investor or advisor conversations. Do not post investor-return language publicly unless counsel confirms the fundraising path.

## Files

- `crowdfunding_campaign.md` - public-facing campaign page, short versions, reward tiers, updates.
- `investor_memo.md` - sober investment-style memo for the starter platform portfolio.
- `product_one_pagers.md` - one-page summaries for the four platform lines.
- `use_of_funds_budget.md` - minimum, practical, and stretch budgets.
- `outreach_messages.md` - email, DM, post, and update templates.
- `risk_disclosures.md` - plain-English risks and boundaries.
- `pitch_deck_outline.md` - slide-by-slide deck structure.

## Important Boundary

Donation/reward crowdfunding and securities fundraising are different. A campaign that offers gratitude, updates, early access, or non-financial perks is not the same as selling equity, debt, revenue share, SAFE notes, or profit participation.

If Ksisti-Puck LLC wants to sell securities to the public, Regulation Crowdfunding generally requires use of a registered funding portal or broker-dealer and formal disclosures. Treat this pack as drafting support, not legal advice.

Current official references:

- SEC Regulation Crowdfunding: https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/exempt-offerings/regulation-crowdfunding
- SEC issuer guidance: https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/small-business-compliance-guides/regulation-crowdfunding-guidance-issuers
