# Use Of Funds Budget

These budgets are conservative operating-capacity budgets, not growth projections.

## Budget Principles

- Buy practical refurbished hardware, not luxury hardware.
- Prefer a thrifted/used desk.
- Fund subscriptions only where they directly improve build, test, design, documentation, deployment, or product polish.
- Keep receipts.
- Separate business expenses from personal spending.
- Avoid long annual commitments until the product focus is clearer.

|---|---:|---|
| Refurbished desktop | $900-$1,300 | RAM/CPU headroom for Android Studio, local servers, browsers, and test runners |

- Stable development workstation.
- Ability to run heavier local builds.
- Basic backup discipline.
- One month to restart product hardening with less friction.

## Subscription Categories

Actual vendors can change. The categories matter more than the brand.

- AI-assisted development and code review.
- Design/prototyping.
- Source control and CI/CD.
- Hosting/deployment.
- Analytics/error monitoring.
- Documentation and writing.
- Video/screen capture.
- Security/password management.
- Backup/storage.
- App store/developer accounts if needed.

## Funding Allocation Rule

Recommended allocation:

- 45-55% hardware/workstation
- 25-35% software subscriptions
- 10-20% demo/product packaging
- 5-15% admin/legal/accounting/contingency

## What This Budget Does Not Claim

This budget does not claim to fully launch all four products. It funds the operating environment required to refine, test, package, and narrow them responsibly.
